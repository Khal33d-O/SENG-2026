# W3.05 acceptance criteria

- My record identifies the requested base/proposal and the actual HEAD/branch of each inspected location.
- I distinguish working, index and committed comparisons using their actual output.
- My chosen review route accounts for the occupied feature branch and retained review work.
- My final review location is clean at the requested proposal, with its full file list inspected.
- My verification cites the actual location, revision, command and bounded result, or an honest execution blocker.
- The preservation check supports my claim about unrelated staged, working and untracked content.
- My PR account describes defect, correction, scope and verification, including one rejected alternative and the remaining review step.
- My individual explanation and assistance level are recorded separately from a partner's successful execution.
